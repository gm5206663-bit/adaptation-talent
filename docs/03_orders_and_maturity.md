# 03 — Orders & Maturity

*How the Talent's depth is measured: ten tracked dimensions instead of one label, four
maturity stages that stay scoped, and the Ten Adaptive Orders — five kinds of development
and five categories of reality.*

---

## One label is not enough (§48)

The first thing the core settles is that "what Order is he?" is an incomplete question.
Every major status tracks **ten dimensions separately**:

| # | Dimension | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Adaptive Order** | What is the deepest category of reality currently accessible? |
| 2 | **Maturity** | How completely is that reach embodied? |
| 3 | **Capacity** | How much change and complexity can be supported? |
| 4 | **Breadth** | How many layers or simultaneous targets can be handled? |
| 5 | **Precision** | How selectively and accurately can adaptation operate? |
| 6 | **Integration** | How harmonious is the complete existence? |
| 7 | **Recursion** | How well can the adaptive method improve itself? |
| 8 | **Authority** | How well can the adapted state be preserved and enforced against interference? |
| 9 | **Current output** | What can the present body, form, resources, and environment actually express? |
| 10 | **Resource condition** | What are the reserves, debts, damage, and missing requirements? |

A holder can be high-Order and low-output, or low-Order and overwhelming. Both are honest
states; collapsing them into a single "power level" is the error this list exists to
prevent.

## Maturity: four stages, always scoped (§48.1)

- **Nascent** — first stable access.
- **Developing** — repeated use and expansion.
- **Mature** — broad, stable, integrated competence.
- **Perfected within current depth** — complete embodiment of the currently accessible scope.

> "Perfected" remains scoped. A deeper world, stronger authority, or new mechanism can
> reveal further development. — §48.1

## Order is not combat power (§48.2)

The core states it with an example that should be pinned above every writer's desk:

> A physically destructive mortal may overpower a nascent divine child in immediate
> output. The child can still possess Divine Order because divinity is genuinely part of
> its existence. — §48.2

Order measures *what kind of reality the holder's existence genuinely touches* — not how
hard they hit. A character with immense energy may lack conceptual access; a subtle
conceptual being may lack city-destroying force. Write both truths at once.

## The Ten Orders

The Orders split into two families (§48.3), and the split is the point:

### I–V — developmental capability (what the holder can *develop*)

| Order | Name | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Foundational** (§50.1) | Adapts within the current natural existence — prenatal and childhood development, defect repair, refinement, learning, regulation, healing, latent-potential realization. *Supernatural results require a valid supernatural possibility.* |
| **II** | **Integrated** (§50.2) | Harmonizes multiple layers — body–mind–soul coordination, bloodline stabilization, multi-energy compatibility, transformation control, bonded-equipment sync, whole-system routing. *Preserves useful differences; never flattens every power into one generic force.* |
| **III** | **Generative** (§50.3) | Constructs genuinely new compatible functions from real substrate — the order where the Talent becomes a builder, not only a refiner. |
| **IV** | **Evolutionary** (§50.4) | **Permanently changes what the holder is** — species evolution is the canonical expression. |
| **V** | **Transcendent** (§50.5) | Discovers or builds a route *beyond the holder's native ceiling* — ascension among them. |

### VI–X — categories of reality (what the holder's existence touches)

| Order | Name | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| **VI** | **Law** (§51) | Meaningful interaction with fundamental operating principles of the current reality. |
| **VII** | **Divine/Authority** (§52) | Genuine divinity, sacred power, divine bodies and souls. |
| **VIII** | **Conceptual** (§53) | Objective concepts and fundamental meanings — *when those are real forces in the cosmology.* |
| **IX** | **Causal** (§54) | Time; fate and destiny; probability and luck — as separate mastery domains. |
| **X** | **Existential** (§55) | Identity, existence and nonexistence, ontological transformation. |

### Overlap is the designed behavior (§48.3)

Orders VI–X are **not a rigid franchise-independent battle ladder**. In one cosmology,
godhood arrives as law + concept + causality in a single ascension; in another, a non-divine
being touches time before divinity. *The universal names classify what is being adapted* —
local cosmologies decide what unlocks together and what it costs.

## Progression, starting state, permanence (§49)

- **Starting Order (§49.1)** depends on species, bloodline, soul quality, conception
  conditions, inherited divinity, natural existential level, world laws, and prenatal
  resources. A genuine divine fetus can begin at Divine Order with **Nascent** maturity and
  **low current output** — high category, small expression, no contradiction.
- **Advancement (§49.2)** requires actual access to a deeper category: evolution,
  experience, comprehension, resources, inheritance, breakthrough, authority, identity
  development, or valid contact. Borrowing a higher power can grant *exposure* without
  establishing permanent Order.
- **Whole-order jumps (§49.3)** are possible when a major existential event genuinely
  supplies every required foundation. There are no universal experience points and no
  fixed ages.
- **Permanence (§49.4)** — a consolidated Order remains part of the holder. *Expression*
  may fall — exhaustion, injury, sealing, world suppression, missing embodiment — but the
  category stays earned.

## The profile in practice

Every module in [`modules/`](../modules/) places its holder on this grid, and the
placement is always visible in the holder's status panel. Three examples of the range:

- **Lan Shen** (Soul Land 1, age six, pre-ring): *Order I — Foundational, early maturity* —
  a farm child whose extraordinary quality is "the completeness and soundness of an
  otherwise ordinary child." See [modules/lan-shen.md](../modules/lan-shen.md) §3.
- **The Eternal OC** (MCU, 7,000 years old): a deliberately split placement — Order III
  Generative/Mature for body and energy; Order II Integrated/Mature for team and equipment;
  Order IV only as a **seed**; Order VI Law **developing** ("uses gravity law expertly but
  does not own it"). See [modules/eternal-oc-mcu.md](../modules/eternal-oc-mcu.md) §10.
- **Mark Stark** (MCU-adjacent): mortal scope at birth → supernatural scope the moment the
  psychic core lives — the framework's rule that the Talent mirrors the holder's tier, never
  leading and never lagging. See
  [modules/mark-stark-heir-study.md](../modules/mark-stark-heir-study.md).

The one-line version of the whole document — from
[`canon/LEVEL-FRAMEWORK.md`](../canon/LEVEL-FRAMEWORK.md):

> The Adaptation Talent is as powerful as the user is. It grows when the user grows. It
> obeys the laws of whatever tier the user occupies — it is perfection of adaptation
> within that tier, never omnipotence beyond it.
