# 04 — Writing It

*The story-use law: how the Talent appears in prose, how it stays secret, how panels and
logs keep it honest, how modules are built beneath it, and how corrections are made. This
is the part a writer uses daily.*

---

## The first rule: it is never the narrator's mechanic (§63)

The complete mechanics — orders, dimensions, state labels — live in foundations, ledgers,
audits, and milestone panels. **Prose shows results:**

> Story prose should show Adaptation Talent through: Breath · Foot placement · Muscle
> memory · Pain recognition · Rhythm … — §63

The working phrase across every serial in the account is **"shown as work, never as a
mechanic"** — and beyond that, *never named in prose at all* (§66.1). Sections §63–§65
then govern pacing and tension: growth is presented at honest speed (§64), and **full-capacity
storytelling (§65)** means the holder being strong is never a reason for a story to be
boring — stakes move to what strength cannot solve.

## Secrecy: the architecture (§66)

The detection and secrecy system is story law, not a suggestion:

- **Default secrecy (§66.1)** — in-world, no character has the name or the concept of the
  Talent. Observers see results, never causes. (One author ruling expands the branch:
  a holder may *privately* know the engine — see Luo Chen's module — but the world still
  sees only results.)
- **What appraisal may show (§66.2)** — measurement devices read the holder's local systems
  (soul power, body condition, output). They show results, not cause. A scan may be
  extraordinary without ever being *explained*.
- **Holder awareness (§66.3)** — what the holder feels of the Talent is bodily and
  instinctive: comfort, steadiness, warmth, unusual recovery, a sense that a pathway is
  wrong. Not diagnosis; not a voice. Where the author has ruled a holder *knows*, the
  knowledge is the holder's own, and the world still never does.
- **Knowledge boundaries (§66.4)** — enemies and institutions require *observation +
  resources + time* to learn anything. No institution may counter the Talent without having
  earned the information. Nothing may be invented for tension that no one has evidence for.

## Panels, logs, modules: the three documents (§69–§71)

### The Definitive Status Panel — §69

A major full panel carries **34 rows**, from identity and checkpoint through adaptive
priorities, foundations (natural body, mind, soul, bloodline), powers and interfaces,
consolidated and developing adaptations, resistance and healing scopes, senses, skills,
relationships, institutional knowledge, weaknesses, recent growth and its causes, the
capability labels (confirmed / conditional / developing / theoretical / unavailable /
dormant / suppressed), comparison with the last checkpoint, and **non-negotiable continuity
locks**. The **evidence rule (§69.1)** sits over all of it: every major ability must
identify its actual source.

Worked full panel: [modules/lan-shen.md](../modules/lan-shen.md) §5 (the 34-row opening
panel at age six). Section §69.3 adds the **delta convention**: full panel rebuilds happen
at major breakthroughs, major corrections, or author request — ordinary chapters append a
**Status Delta** instead.

### The Adaptation Log — §70

One entry per meaningful milestone, each written in eight parts:

**70.1 Cause · 70.2 Starting foundation · 70.3 Immediate response · 70.4 Whole-existence
effect · 70.5 Resource cost · 70.6 Current state · 70.7 Continuity comparison · 70.8
Later proof.**

Every claim carries a state label from the §22.1 ladder. Worked examples live in
[modules/lan-shen-log.md](../modules/lan-shen-log.md) (Entry 0 baseline + Entry 1
awakening) and [modules/devouring-dragon-log.md](../modules/devouring-dragon-log.md).

### The Character- and Universe-Specific Module — §71

The module template has seven parts — the shape every file in
[`modules/`](../modules/) follows:

1. **71.1 Identity** — holder, universe, branch, timeline, starting conditions
2. **71.2 Local reality** — the world's genuine laws, and what the Talent may refine here
3. **71.3 Starting adaptation** — what the Talent has actually produced by the opening
4. **71.4 Current source architecture** — the separation ledger: what is the Talent, what
   is the world's gift, what is learned, worn, contracted, transformed
5. **71.5 Current ledger** — state rows as of the checkpoint
6. **71.6 Progression path** — laws only, never plot
7. **71.7 Continuity** — what must survive into the next checkpoint

Modules sit **beneath** the universal core and never rewrite it (§71; §76). The Blue Silver
module states the family rule for identity files — a module must "hold BOTH truths at
once" when a premise contains an apparent contradiction (say, a real elemental mutation
*and* an all-effecting Talent), instead of choosing a half that is easier to write.

## Canon, timelines, and the divergence ledger (§62)

- **Meta knowledge is not the Talent (§62.1).** A reincarnated holder's former-life
  knowledge is theirs alone — and by locked law, it **becomes less reliable as the branch
  diverges**. Every change the holder makes rots the book they remember. (This is the
  engine behind the account's divergence ledgers.)
- **Canon-first causality (§62.2).** The holder is inserted *around* canon; canon changes
  only where established actions genuinely change a cause — never to force an outcome back
  (§62.3), never by making the holder irrational, never by inventing machinery.
- **Universe separation** — nothing leaks between serials because two holders share the
  Talent (§62.5). The Talent is the one law; each holder's powers, souls, and worlds remain
  their own (§1).

## The twin continuity law (§61)

Two failure modes are banned with equal force:

- **No nerfing.** Previously earned abilities and legitimately established history are not
  erased or reduced to make a new scene work.
- **No unearned inflation.** Nothing appears without the causal sufficiency test (§22.2)
  being able to pass.

The same principle governs corrections (§68): a correction **repairs the causal explanation
without silently erasing legitimate development** — and the correction protocol requires
stating what changed, why, which continuities are affected, and how contradictions are
repaired.

## The wrong portrayals table (§73)

The core keeps an exact-corrections table. A sample of the entries every holder's writer
should keep at hand:

| Wrong portrayal | Correct rule |
|---|---|
| "Adaptation Talent is basically fast learning" | It develops the complete holder across body, mind, soul, identity, energy, powers, resistance, and valid interfaces |
| "The Talent told him the answer" | The holder experiences instinct, sensation, inference, or consciously developed self-awareness |
| "He survived once, so he is immune" | One survival may establish information, emergency compensation, or partial resistance |
| "Cannot create from nothing, so it can never create powers" | It may construct new abilities through valid possibility, input, substrate, resources, and integration |
| "He saw the technique and mastered everything" | First reproduction, conditioning, applied mastery, and creative mastery are separate |
| "A new breakthrough upgrades every statistic equally" | Whole-stage compatibility is universal; raw growth remains role- and mechanism-specific |
| "Overload makes the scene tense" | Overload exists only when a real excess, incompatibility, damage, or resource cause exists |
| "He respects consent, so he cannot protect anyone" | Consent governs invasive personal action, not safe non-invasive rescue |

## The silent audit (§74) and the final summary (§75)

Before any chapter, panel, comparison, or visual ships, §74's silent pre-writing audit
runs: continuity first (right branch, right checkpoint, right body, all permanent
adaptations retained, dormant abilities labeled), then portrayal, then causal sufficiency,
then secrecy. And §75 closes the core with fifty rules "that must never be forgotten" —
beginning with *exists from conception*, *not a system*, *always operating but not always
increasing raw output*, *affects the complete existence*, and *cannot create from nothing
never means can only improve old abilities*.
