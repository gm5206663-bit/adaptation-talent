# 02 — How It Works

*The operating architecture of the Talent: what it touches, how it grows, how fast, what
it costs, and where it stops. Every numbered reference points into
[`canon/MASTER-FOUNDATION.md`](../canon/MASTER-FOUNDATION.md).*

---

## The three domains (§10)

The Talent's reach is defined as three nested domains — and the clean separation between
them prevents most accidental power inflation.

| Domain | What it covers | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** (§10.1) | The holder's own continuous existence — body, mind, soul, senses, energy pathways, instincts, will | Deepest, most automatic; the default country of the Talent |
| **Interface** (§10.2) | Bonded weapons, artifacts, symbiotes, spirits, contracts, technology the holder genuinely connects with | Works **through** the real connection; it does not seize the object or replace its nature |
| **Authored derivative** (§10.3) | Beings and systems the holder deliberately creates through their own earned capability and authority | The far horizon: a mastered holder may grow adaptive lineages, constructs, territories — products of earned reach, never of this era's convenience |

**The identity-convergence function (§11.1)** ties the domains together: everything the
Talent works on tends toward *being the holder* — integrated, personalized, continuous. And
there is **no detachable signature (§11.2)**: no scan, appraisal, or audit can isolate "the
Talent" as an object, because it is distributed through a whole existence rather than
installed at a point.

## Modes: one law, many faces (§12)

The Talent expresses through twelve named modes, and knowing them prevents the classic
writing error of treating it as a single gear:

maintenance · developmental · directed · reactive · anticipatory · integrative · recovery ·
exploratory · environmental · relational · evolutionary · recursive.

A quiet chapter is the **maintenance** mode, not a dormant Talent. A fight is **reactive**;
a convalescence is **recovery**; meeting a new world is **environmental**; loving someone is
**relational**. The mode list is a diagnostic tool: if a scene seems to show "nothing
happening," find which mode is running — something always is (§12; §57.3 is explicit that
being ambushed mid-maintenance is not a design flaw but a real danger to a real holder).

## The causal cycle, without a mind inside (§13)

The v2.0 language for the old "observe → analyze → adjust" cycle is:

> **non-conscious causal registration → embodied correction → integration.** — §1.1

What actually happens when the Talent meets an experience: a real effect registers against
the holder's real state (no thought, no voice); the body/mind/soul produce **corrections**
that are physical, instinctive, and gradual; and corrections become **integrated** — part of
the holder, not maintained by effort. Everything else in this document is that loop, run
under different conditions.

**Exposure, information, and evidence (§14)** grade the inputs: a glancing observation is
worth less than sustained contact; hearsay is not evidence; measurable experience — pain,
pressure, success, failure — is the currency. **The survival window (§15)** governs first
contact: a first attack can be decisive, and no adaptation can outrun what kills it before
the loop has anything to work with (§57.1).

## Speed and growth pressure (§16)

- **Growth is nonlinear (§16.1).** Pressure, novelty, and genuine need accelerate; ease and
  routine slow it — but *slowness is not dormancy*: consolidation continues.
- **Injury is not required (§16.2).** The Talent does not need damage to grow; it needs
  exposed reality.
- **Diminishing returns (§16.3)** cap runaway loops: once a challenge becomes routine, it
  stops paying. Growth then shifts to precision, breadth, or new pressure — never to raw
  escalation by mere repetition.

The companion laws in [`canon/LEVEL-FRAMEWORK.md`](../canon/LEVEL-FRAMEWORK.md) name the
inputs: **difficulty** (greater challenges → greater potential), **exposure** (repetition
required), **compatibility** (closer to the holder's foundation → easier), and **stress**
(meaningful pressure required; no challenge, no major adaptation).

## Memory, recursion, capacity (§17–§19)

- **Adaptive memory (§17)** is the keeper of everything earned: stance corrections, pattern
  recognition, immunity signatures, emotional traces. It is *not* conscious recall — the
  holder may have hands that remember what their head forgot. (The MCU module builds an
  entire story spine on this distinction; see [module](../modules/eternal-oc-mcu.md).)
- **Recursion (§18)** is the Talent working on its own adaptations: refining the refinement,
  integrating integrations. It is why long-lived holders deepen instead of plateauing — and
  why recursion is tracked as its own dimension of the profile (§48).
- **Capacity, bandwidth, and parallel operation (§19)** are real and finite. A holder can
  adapt in many fields, but attention, energy, and recovery bound how much is being
  *actively* built at once. Overload is a real failure mode (§21), not a dramatic flourish.

## The resource economy (§20)

Nothing here is free, and the ledger is concrete:

- **The ordinary resource hierarchy (§20.1)** ranks what the Talent's work runs on:
  the holder's body and recovery first, then energy and reserve systems, then materials,
  information, time, and external aid.
- **Resource debt (§20.2)** is borrowable and repayable — the body overdraws for an
  emergency, and the bill arrives as exhaustion, healing, sleep, or hunger.
- **Energy conversion (§20.3)** must respect conversion losses: no system here creates
  energy; it routes, refines, and stores what genuinely exists.

**Integration, bottlenecks, and overload (§21):** new gains must pass integration gates; a
foreign power may take years to seat; pushing past capacity produces overload with real
symptoms. **Bottlenecks and scoped perfection (§24):** progress concentrates at the current
bottleneck, and a holder can be *perfect within a scope* long before general mastery —
"perfected" always carries its scope.

## The state ladder — every claim graded (§22.1)

Before any ability is described as "mastered," it sits somewhere on this ladder, and mastery
claims must name their scope:

| State | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unknown | No usable contact or information |
| 1 | Registered | A real effect or opportunity has been noticed |
| 2 | Provisionally mapped | Some causal pattern is understood, with uncertainty |
| 3 | Emergency compensation | A temporary survival or function response exists |
| 4 | Developing adaptation | Measurable improvement exists but is unreliable or narrow |
| 5 | Stable adaptation | A repeatable response has consolidated |
| 6 | Specialized mastery | Highly efficient performance exists within a defined scope |
| 7 | Family generalization | Related variants can be handled through a broader principle |
| 8 | Principle integration | The underlying mechanism is part of the holder's stable system |
| 9 | Foundational evolution | The adaptation has permanently changed what the holder is |
| 10 | Sovereign personal expression | The holder can enforce a mature personal version within established reach |

**No state means universal absoluteness.** The ladder's twin is the **causal sufficiency
test (§22.2)** — eight questions asked *before* any new ability is declared:

1. **Possibility** — does the reality permit a route?
2. **Input** — what was inherited, experienced, observed, learned, acquired?
3. **Substrate** — what body, soul, energy, power, artifact, or law can carry it?
4. **Resources** — what matter, energy, time, recovery, or authority paid for it?
5. **Construction** — what actually changed?
6. **Integration** — how are control and compatibility maintained?
7. **Evidence** — what feat or consequence confirms current function?
8. **Scope** — what remains outside the adaptation?

Fail a step and the result must carry an honest label: *potential, seed, developing,
conditional, theoretical,* or *unavailable*. The same test "prevents both artificial nerfing
and unearned inflation" (§22.2) — which is its whole design.

## Growth law, in order of the document

- **Cumulative development (§23)** — nothing earned is silently lost; later perception,
  difficulty, speed, cost, and tactics must reflect earlier gains. (The account's phrase:
  *the gains-ledger law*.)
- **Innovation ladder (§25)** — new abilities pass seed → unstable → controlled → stable,
  and not every exposure becomes a power (§25.3). Recombination and synthesis are real
  (§25.1), and honest labeling of seeds is required (§25.2).
- **Copying, reconstruction, ownership (§26)** — observation can produce reconstruction
  under the one-observation rule; *ownership* is different: what a holder makes of a
  technique through their own substrate is theirs, and what they merely saw stays shallow.
- **Learning and comprehension (§27)** — understanding can accelerate; knowledge cannot be
  downloaded; one-observation reproduction applies only with complete information and
  prerequisites.
- **Skills and practice (§28)** — the Talent makes *real* practice unusually valuable; it
  never creates practice that did not occur. **Automatic training stays separate (§28;
  §5)** — that is IATS's job, not this Talent's.
- **Breakthroughs (§29)** — whole-stage events: they recalibrate the entire holder
  (§29.1), require a minimum stable foundation (§29.2), need post-breakthrough development
  (§29.3), and **must show observable consequences** (§29.4).

## The complete existence (Part IV, §32–§47)

The Talent works everywhere. The master walks the whole map: prenatal development and the
mother–child connection (§32), the physical body (§33), healing and lost structures (§34),
metabolism, sleep, aging, lifespan (§35), appearance and presence (§36), mind, intelligence,
memory, wisdom (§37), emotion, personality, desire, trauma (§38), soul, spirit, will,
identity (§39), senses, instinct, danger-awareness (§40), genetics, species, bloodlines
(§41), energy systems and absorption (§42), resistance and immunity architecture (§43),
combat expression and battle growth (§44), fusion, symbiosis, contracts, relationships
(§45), teams, teaching, leadership, organizations (§46), and technology, environment,
territory (§47).

Two laws from this part deserve to travel with every module:

- **Emotions are not impurities (§9.5).** The Talent regulates; it does not delete. Fear,
  grief, and love are inputs of the highest quality, not obstacles to be optimized away.
- **Free will outranks optimization (§9.1).** The holder may choose a path the Talent would
  not choose; that choice is law at the top of the priority architecture — above intentions,
  emergencies, and even survival (§9.2–§9.4).

## Where it stops (§57–§60)

Failure is designed in, not patched around:

- **Genuine failure conditions (§57)** — overwhelming first attack, missing information,
  maintenance intervals, incompatible demands, starvation of needs, detection by what
  cannot be veiled.
- **Counter-adaptation (§58)** — sealing, suppression, and nullification exist in the
  multiverse; the Talent can be contained the way anything real can.
- **Theft and "absolute" effects (§59)** — copy-thieves, corruptors, and possessors are
  real oppositions, each bounded by the same causal sufficiency test.
- **Absolute universal limits (§60)** — even at the highest Order: information requires a
  source; output requires real capacity; a first attack can be decisive; the holder can
  err; stronger realities contain deeper challenges; anchorless erasure can end them; and
  the Talent "cannot force love, loyalty, consent, or moral agreement … cannot freeze the
  world while only the holder grows."
