## Near-neighbours — deliberately *not* holders

Keeping these separate is the same law as §5's separation; they are filed here so no future
session merges them by accident:

- **Lin Hao** (adaptive_prodigy, Soul Land 3) — his *martial soul itself* is named
  **Adaptation**: "a fight you survive is a fight you keep." That is a martial-soul mechanic
  with its own canon divergence, explicitly marked as **not** the Adaptation Talent
  (`lan_shen/foundation/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`: *"Adaptation talent? No — explicit divergence
  from Lin Hao"*).
- **The Genesis Codex's Adaptation Core** — the Codex's self-improving meta-system, a
  sibling concept filed in [`canon/GENESIS-CODEX-ADAPTIVE.md`](../canon/GENESIS-CODEX-ADAPTIVE.md);
  it adapts *a document system*, not a being.
- **IATS and IODS** — the Infinite Automatic Training System and the Infinite Optimization
  & Development System are separate cheats with their own mechanics; the Talent must never
  absorb, imitate, or subsume them (§5).

## Reading the register

- **Module status varies on purpose.** Most holders have dedicated module files, verbatim
  and complete. Two holders (Evo, Vihaan) are currently documented through the craft layer;
  their register rows say so rather than pretending a module exists.
- **Every module quotes its author rulings verbatim.** The author's word outranks every
  document (authority order, §1) — so the rulings travel inside the files.
- **Modules never rewrite the core.** All sixteen sit beneath
  [`canon/MASTER-FOUNDATION.md`](../canon/MASTER-FOUNDATION.md) per §71/§76.
