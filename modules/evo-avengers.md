## 1. The holder

The account's oldest Adaptation Talent run. Evo is a full Avenger and co-protagonist —
"a multi-thousand-ton powerhouse whose presence is SUBSTANTIAL in every episode" — with
telekinesis, psychic ability, energy transfer, empathic projection, and a gamma-state.
His threads across the run: his own intro fight (E1: A.I.M. drone), his power-development
and suit-build lines, his thematic contributions ("nothing is absolute"; "the mind wins,
not power"; "aim, don't just swing"), and an arc from reckless to wise.

## 6. Why these laws exist — the failures that bought them

- The **sequence failure** (the S1E9 era: episodes silently skipped, one mislabeled, a
  "next episode" off by ten) became **Law 4**, the **Sequence Lock**, a required
  master episode↔chapter map, and the chapter-number invariant: *chapter file number =
  reading order = episode order — relocate files, don't let the numbers lie.*
- The **suppression era** (an "≤30% Evo budget" and an agency gate that kept the OC small)
  was diagnosed as *underselling* and abolished in v4 — replaced by equal-weight co-lead
  rules.
- The skill's own record runs to its last update (**Post-S1E13, two-axis model**). Read the
  file itself for the live edge; this module does not move it.
