## 1. Identity at the opening (§71.1 in miniature)

- Twenty-four years old when Episode 1 begins; IPS officer posted in the district
  containing Panchner and the haveli.
- The surviving son of two powerful Sheshnaag-clan parents who died in an ancient
  supernatural attack; adopted as a baby by the Rahejas, who do not know his nature.
- Om Kaka — the trusted driver — is secretly an old Naag who has protected him since
  childhood; his knowledge is incomplete.
- He does not know: **his adoption, his name, his Naag nature, the Adaptation Talent, or
  Mahadev's choice.** (Control book, knowledge firewall.)

## 2. The talent law as locked for him (verbatim)

> - Gaurav awakens on his twenty-fifth birthday several episodes after Episode 1 — not
>   during the premiere.
> - **Adaptation Talent has spent twenty-four years perfectly integrating both parental
>   Sheshnaag bloodlines.**
> - His awakened form is a sacred golden five-hooded **Panchmukhi Maha Naag**.
> - **All accumulated raw power is available and safely integrated immediately.**
> - **Instinctive control does not provide trained technique, Naag-world history or
>   omniscient knowledge.**
> - Mahadev silently chose him at birth as a future sacred protector. Divine favor gives
>   purpose, not automatic victory.
> - … His initial raw output exceeds Shivanya's normal peak. Shivanya can equal it through
>   the Naagmani or direct Mahadev/Tandav empowerment.
> - He can later wield, protect, channel and control the Naagmani, but the gem is not the
>   source of his innate power.
> - After awakening he is **ageless against natural aging, not invulnerable or unkillable.**
> — `practice/naagin-s1-control-book.md` §"Awakening rules"

Read against the universal core: a twenty-four-year prenatal-through-adult integration is
§23 + §32 + §41 at a lifetime's scale; "instinctive control ≠ technique or knowledge" is
§27/§28; "purpose, not automatic victory" is §60's limit list in a devotional key; "ageless,
not unkillable" is §35 + §57's honesty.

## 3. The pre-awakening accounting

Everything before the awakening is **integration, not expression** — the Talent's
twenty-four-year project is the boy himself: two bloodlines made one, a body and mind that
read as extraordinary-but-human (magnetism that "can create awareness or attraction; it
cannot compel consent, loyalty or love"), and a knowledge firewall that holds absolutely
until awakening. The world sees an unusually magnetic young officer; nobody sees a cause.
