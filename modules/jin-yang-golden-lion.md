## 2. L-04 — the Adaptation Talent law (verbatim)

> ## L-04 — ADAPTATION TALENT LAW (author law, womb-born)
> > "he have adaption telent from birth mother womb"
>
> 1. **Above-ceiling absorption:** he may absorb rings past L-03 ceilings IF (a) donor–soul
>    compatibility is high AND (b) his body holds. Failure conditions stay lethal.
> 2. **Full trait transfer, re-expressed:** donor traits do not copy-paste; they are
>    re-expressed as **golden-lion versions** of themselves (his soul wears every trait its
>    own way).
> 3. **Bloodline refinement:** each compatible donor purifies/strengthens the lion bloodline.
> 4. **Cultivation efficiency:** his body adapts to training load — sustained ~3.5 ranks/year
>    explains rank 23 at age 11 from innate 5. `[design-signed]`
> 5. **COSTS (never waived):** every absorption is life-risk; body-load caps apply;
>    conflicting …
> — `soul_land_2_new/foundation/AUTHORS_LAW.md` (L-04)

Directly above it, the serial states the framing the law exists for:

> Our hero **exceeds the ladder** — that is what L-04 exists to justify. Signed exception,
> ledger 006. — same file

**Read against the universal core:** item 1 is §42.1 (access rules are real; the Talent
does not substitute for them), item 2 is §26 (what the holder makes of what they take is
*re-expressed* through their own substrate — ownership, not copy-paste), item 3 is §41
(bloodline refinement through valid causes), item 4 is §16.1 + §28 (real training made
unusually valuable), and item 5 is §20 (costs never waived).

## 4. The edge-case register (verbatim)

> ## §3 ADAPTATION TALENT — edge-case register (extends AUTHORS_LAW L-04)
> 1. Same-species donor (ring 1) = maximal compatibility = maximal refinement → bloodline
>    jump signed.
> 2. Cross-species donor (ring 2 drake) = traits re-expressed as lion versions; foreign
>    aspects (scales, dragon frame) are NOT worn raw — they translate or fail.
> 3. Donor ceiling scales with his body: at rank 23 his frame held 500y→1,000y steps; future
>    donors priced per rank, never free (L-04.5).
> 4. Element conflicts (fire vs ice, light vs dark) still kill; adaptation widens doors, it
>    does not delete walls.
> 5. No trait invention: … Anything not in the donor does not appear. Every future card must
>    name its donor-trait lineage.
> — `soul_land_2_new/foundation/THE_LION_MODULE.md` §3

Item 4 is the sharpest local statement of a universal rule (**§24.4 / §60**: the Talent
widens doors; it does not delete walls), and item 5 is §22.2's causal sufficiency test
applied to every future ring.
